What We Offer

Physical Security &
Journey Management

Casper & Saint delivers a comprehensive range of technology-enhanced security services tailored to the unique risk profile of every client.

Armed Guarding & Escort Duties

Professional armed security personnel for premises protection and high-value escort missions.

Journey Management

End-to-end route planning, risk assessment, and vehicle escort for safe personnel movement.

Close Protection Services

Executive and VIP protection by trained close-protection officers with threat assessment expertise.

CCTV Monitoring & Installation

Full-service CCTV system design, installation, and 24/7 remote monitoring with AI-assisted analytics.

Maritime Security Advisory

Specialist maritime security services, liaising with naval authorities to protect offshore and port assets.

Anti-Bomb Squad (EOD/CBRN)

Event Site Safety & Security

Medical Response Team

Trained first-responders embedded within security deployments to handle medical emergencies on-site.

Freight Logistics & Security

Secure cargo escort, logistics risk assessment, and freight protection across road and maritime corridors in Nigeria and beyond.
