Smart Guard System

Technology-Driven Security

Our Smart Guard model integrates real-time digital reporting, GPS-tracked patrol management, biometric attendance, and cloud-based command dashboards.

01

Real-Time SITREP Reporting

Guards submit digital situation reports via QR-code checkpoints, timestamped data accessible from any device.

02

GPS Patrol Tracking

Every patrol is geo-tagged and logged in real time. Supervisors receive instant alerts for missed checkpoints or boundary breaches.

03

Operations Dashboard

Clients and management access live dashboards showing guard deployment, incident logs, attendance, and performance KPIs, anytime, anywhere.

04

Biometric Access Control

Fingerprint and facial recognition systems deployed at client facilities eliminate buddy-punching and unauthorised access.

05

CCTV & Remote Monitoring

AI-assisted video analytics detect anomalies, perimeter breaches, crowd buildups, and unauthorised access before incidents escalate.

06

Incident & Alarm Response

Automated alert chains route incidents to the nearest response team. Digital reports filed instantly for audit and compliance purposes.
